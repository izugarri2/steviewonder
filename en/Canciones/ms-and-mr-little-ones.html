Ms. & Mr. Little Ones


1995 album Natural Wonder


Composer * Stevie Wonder and Carmine Coppola
Produced and arranged * Stevie Wonder

"Ms. & Mr. Little One" is featured on the live recording that Stevie Wonder made in Osaka, Japan, in 1994, for his 1995 live album *Natural Wonder*. The song uses a respectful and affectionate term to address children. The lyrics describe how little ones imitate or are exposed to the behaviors of adults—such as violence, obscene language, or drug use—which cause them serious harm; the song also offers an apology for the state of the world while encouraging the new generation to build a better one.

In the English-speaking world, "little ones" is a common, affectionate term for young children, while "Ms." and "Mr." are formal titles used between adults—though here, they are employed with an ironic, solemn, or endearing tone to lend these children a sense of "importance" as individuals.
